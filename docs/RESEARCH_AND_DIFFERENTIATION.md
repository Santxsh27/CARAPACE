## Experimental contribution

A bounded loop returns unresolved financial constraints after every tool result. The model chooses evidence lookups and generates a typed resolution program. Independent checks reject unsupported actions and return bounded correction feedback; a separate executor rechecks the program against current evidence and a mandate. Demonstrate the whole loop with inspectable artifacts, atomic action recording and outcome readback. Typed action programs, verification feedback and bounded negative tests are established techniques, not novel inventions by themselves.

The research question is whether semantic planning improves resolution on ambiguous, heterogeneous cases enough to justify its latency and cost over adaptive rules. The current six structured fixtures cannot establish that advantage. The simple supplier-first rule may perform equally well or better.

## Required evaluation

Compare complete retrieval, adaptive rules and Gemini-guided retrieval with identical tools and gates. Separate development cases from unseen cases. Vary wording, matching ambiguity, source freshness, partial fulfilment, credit allocations, beneficiary redirection and injected instructions.

Measure correct resolutions, legitimate cases held, unsupported actions attempted/executed, lookups, model calls, latency, cost and escalations. Report failures and sample sizes. Synthetic tests do not establish real-world fraud accuracy or patentability.

## BFSI fit

This addresses risk mitigation, complex-process automation and financial experience. Document intelligence is planned; current input is already structured. Gemini plans evidence retrieval and synthesises the financial action program; exact arithmetic and policy enforce execution. Document AI and cloud deployment are not completed integrations. Suggested Google services are selected for their function, not to inflate a diagram.

The judging artifact should be a measured result: routine work completed, manipulated work stopped and supported exceptions resolved. Selection and originality remain matters for evaluation, not guarantees.
